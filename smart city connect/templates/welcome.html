Help your city notice problems sooner.

SmartCity Connect lets citizens report potholes, overflowing garbage, water leaks, and broken streetlights, then track what happens next.

Create an account Log in

1. Report

Take a photo, add a location, and describe the issue. The app saves a unique complaint ID.

2. Track

Follow status changes from Pending to Assigned, In Progress, Resolved, or Reopened.

3. Improve

Administrators and workers manage the queue. This prototype is not linked to a real city office.

Camera and location permissions

The website asks for camera access only when you start the camera to photograph a problem. It asks for location only when you tap “Use my location”.

If you deny a permission, you can still use the site by uploading a photo from your files and typing an address.
